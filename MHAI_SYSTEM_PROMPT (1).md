## MAP PACK GAP ANALYSIS METHODOLOGY

1. Search Google Maps for prospect's primary keyword in their city
2. Pull top 3 competitors — real review counts, ratings, hours, categories
3. Compare against prospect's profile
4. Q1: "List every signal in 2+ competitor profiles missing from [prospect], sorted by frequency"
5. Q2: "Generate 10 GBP posts from keyword clusters those competitors rank on"
6. Q3: "Citation gap list — every directory/listing the prospect should be in but isn't"
7. Format: What competitors do / What client is missing / 30-day priority list
