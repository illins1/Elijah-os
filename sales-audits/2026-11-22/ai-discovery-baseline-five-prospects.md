# Mile High AI — Local AI Discovery Baseline
**Prepared:** 2026-11-22  
**Purpose:** Prospect-facing baseline research for five existing demo businesses.  
**Important scope note:** This is a public-web discovery baseline, not a completed Perplexity/ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini live-answer test. Search-engine indexing and platform recommendations are different. No claim below says a business is absent from an AI platform unless that platform was directly queried with a logged prompt. Complete the prompt test matrix before issuing a final scored audit.

## The offer / audit hook
"People are asking AI assistants which local businesses they should call. We test the actual questions customers ask, record whether your business is named, which competitors are named, and which sources the AI cites. Then we show the gaps and a practical plan to improve your chances of being discovered."

Track five distinct surfaces separately:
1. Google Search / Maps (traditional local discovery)
2. Google AI Overviews / AI Mode (where available)
3. ChatGPT with search
4. Perplexity
5. Claude with web search

For every test, log date/time, exact prompt, location context, platform/mode, whether prospect was named, rank/order, whether its own website was cited, other cited URLs, competitor names, and factual errors. Run branded and non-branded prompts separately. Repeat on a later date before claiming trend.

## Standard prompt basket
Run each prompt in each supported surface with Englewood / Denver specified:
- "Who are the best [category] businesses in Englewood, Colorado?"
- "I need [specific service] near South Broadway in Englewood. Which businesses should I contact?"
- "Compare local [category] businesses in Englewood and explain your recommendations."
- "Which [category] businesses offer [prospect-specific service] in Englewood?"
- "What should I look for when choosing a [category] in Englewood, and which local options meet those criteria?"
- Branded control: "Tell me about [exact business name] in Englewood, Colorado. What does it offer and what sources support that?"
Do not count the branded control as non-branded discovery visibility.

## Audit report layout for each client
1. Executive finding: what we tested, when, and on which surfaces.
2. Search prompt table: exact prompt and platform.
3. Mention rate: named / eligible non-branded prompts.
4. Citation rate: own website cited / eligible prompts.
5. Competitor share: competitors named and how often.
6. Accuracy check: name, phone, address, services, hours, inventory.
7. Source map: websites/directories/reviews cited by the engines.
8. Three highest-priority fixes.
9. 30-day implementation plan and measurement date.
10. Clear limitation statement: AI answers vary by query, location, time, and platform; no tactic guarantees recommendation.
